# Joint and Individual Filing

## Overview

A bankruptcy case is filed either by one debtor or jointly by two spouses. Circumstances change after a case is opened — the client marries or separates, the couple decides late whether to file together, or the debtors were entered in the wrong order — so firm staff can correct who the debtors are from the workflow's settings without starting the case again. These pages cover swapping the two debtors on a joint case and converting a case between joint and individual filing.

## Topics

- [Swapping Debtors](./swapping-debtors.md) — trading Debtor 1 and Debtor 2 on a joint case, what moves with each debtor on the schedules, and the warnings shown before you confirm.
- [Converting Between Joint and Individual Filing](./converting-filing-type.md) — adding a spouse to an individual case or removing one from a joint case, what changes on the case and the schedules, and what is left as it is.
