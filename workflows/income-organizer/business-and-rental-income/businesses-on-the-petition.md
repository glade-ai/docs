# Businesses on the Petition (Form 101 Part 3)

## Overview

Part 3 of the voluntary petition (Form 101) lists the debtor's sole proprietorships. When a business income source is added in the Income Organizer, Glade adds that business to Part 3 on the case's in-progress questionnaires, so the petition and the organizer list the same businesses. This page covers how a business is matched to a row your team already typed, which names reach the petition, and what happens when your team removes a business from Part 3.

## Key Behaviors

- **A business your team already typed is linked, not duplicated.** Before adding a row, Glade looks for a Part 3 row with the same business name. Names are compared without regard to capitalization, spacing, punctuation, or a trailing legal designator such as LLC, Inc, Corp, Co, Ltd, or PLLC. "Munchkin Soft Play" and "Munchkin Soft Play LLC" are treated as one business, and the organizer's business is linked to the existing row.
- **Matching never drops or reorders words.** Names that differ by actual words — "Seal Team One / RMKA LLC" and "RMKA LLC", for example — are not linked automatically. Review them and remove the extra row if they are the same business.
- **The organizer's own labels stay off the petition.** Labels the organizer adds to a business's name — "Business - …", "Business, profession, or farm - …", "… (Business)", "Profit and Loss of …" — are ignored when matching, and are not written into the business name that prints on the petition. The organizer itself still shows the source under the name it had.
- **Businesses named only after an income label are held back.** A business whose name is only gig-platform or self-employment words — "Uber", "Uber Driver", "Self Employed", "Amazon Royalties" — is not added to Part 3, because on a petition it reads as a business called "Uber". Give the business its real name in the organizer and it is added.
- **Businesses with no name, and rental sources, are not added to Part 3.**
- **Removing a business from Part 3 sticks.** When your team removes an organizer business's row from Part 3, Glade stops sending that business to the petition. Previously the row came back the next time the organizer updated the case.

## Edge Cases & Limitations

- **A removed business cannot be brought back from the questionnaire.** Once a business has been removed from Part 3, later organizer changes are not sent to Part 3 for it. If your team removed a business by mistake, contact Glade to have it restored.
- **Renaming after the business reaches Part 3.** If a business that is already on Part 3 is later renamed to an income label, or its name is cleared, its existing row stays on the petition. Remove it by hand if it should not be there.
- Businesses that reached Part 3 before this behavior existed may carry an organizer label in their name, or may have been added as a second row beside one your team typed. Check Part 3 on cases in progress for twin rows and label-named businesses.
