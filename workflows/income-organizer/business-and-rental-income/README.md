# Business and Rental Income

## Overview

Business and rental income sources in the Income Organizer hold their own profit & loss statements, and the figures on those statements feed the business, professional, and rental income line of Schedule I (line 8a). These docs cover how sources are added, how statements are read and bound to businesses, and how line 8a is built from them.

## Topics

- [Profit & Loss Statements](./profit-and-loss-statements.md) — how statements are uploaded, read line by line, corrected, and deleted.
- [Schedule I Line 8a](./schedule-i-line-8a.md) — how line 8a is built from statements and month entries across every business on the case.
- [Matching Statements to Businesses](./matching-statements-to-businesses.md) — binding a statement to a business by folder or printed name, and creating a business from a statement.
- [Adding Business and Rental Sources](./adding-business-sources.md) — the add-source dialog for your team and for clients, business details, and which contact a business belongs to.
