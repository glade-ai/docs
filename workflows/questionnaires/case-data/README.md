# Case Data

## Overview

Questionnaires share information with the case record: synced fields and lists read from it and write back to it, so creditors, assets, and household details entered once reach every form on the case. These pages cover how that sync behaves, how to bring a questionnaire back in step with the case record, and what happens when a questionnaire moves to a newer template version.

## Topics

- [Case Data Sync](./case-data-sync.md) — synced fields, 341 meeting and confirmation hearing details from court notices, several questions tied to one case detail, turning sync off, entity-bound lists, blank rows, deleting and restoring entities, removed import actions, and chapter switches.
- [Re-opening and Re-syncing](./reopening-and-resync.md) — re-opening a completed questionnaire, the Get back in sync preview, and Compare case data.
- [Template Upgrades](./template-upgrades.md) — release notes, the upgrade prompt, and what an upgrade does to case data, sync, and household details.
- [Swapping Primary and Spouse](./swapping-primary-and-spouse.md) — what moves when the primary debtor and spouse trade roles on a joint case, the answers that stay put, and the reversed-names warning.
