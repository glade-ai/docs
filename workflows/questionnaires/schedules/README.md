# Schedule Tools

## Overview

The Bankruptcy Schedules questionnaire carries tools and behaviors specific to individual schedules and forms — property and liens on Schedule A/B, exemptions on Schedule C, creditors and the mailing matrix, income on Schedule I, the means test, and the Chapter 13 plan. These pages cover each one.

## Topics

- [Exemptions Calculator](./exemptions-calculator.md) — the exemptions panel, equity and joint limits, the Schedule C homestead answer, the exemptions agent, and the Texas exemptions schedule.
- [Schedule A/B Property](./property.md) — liens and equity, the Property summary, Unknown values, and how Part 3 and line 19 print.
- [Creditors](./creditors.md) — adding creditors, duplicate status, alphabetized schedules, and the creditor matrix.
- [Creditor Deduplication](./creditor-deduplication.md) — when creditor rows are linked as duplicates automatically, and which row is kept.
- [Schedule I and Income](./income.md) — importing Income Organizer figures and itemizing lines 8f and 8h.
- [Means Test](./means-test.md) — non-consumer Chapter 7 cases and secured debt deductions.
- [Chapter 13 Plan Calculator](./chapter-13-plan-calculator.md) — plan elections, lump sums, unsecured pool, arrearage cures, and generated plans.
- [Chapter 13 Plan Claims](./chapter-13-plan-claims.md) — which questionnaire the plan reads, and keeping duplicate creditors off the plan.
- [Chapter 13 Attorney Fees](./chapter-13-attorney-fees.md) — paying the fee balance after prepetition payments, and Middle District of Florida MMM and monitoring fees.
- [Chapter 13 Plan Districts](./chapter-13-plan-districts.md) — the districts whose local plan forms can be generated, and what to check by hand on each.
