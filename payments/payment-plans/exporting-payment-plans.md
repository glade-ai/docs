# Exporting Payment Plans

## Overview

The Payment Plans section of the transactions dashboard can be downloaded as a spreadsheet, so a firm can review its plans — particularly the ones falling behind — outside Glade or hand the figures to a bookkeeper.

## Key Behaviors

- The export lists one row per plan, with the same columns as the on-screen table plus the client's email address: contact, email, total invoice amount, the date the plan was created, paid so far, amount remaining, installment size, last payment date, last payment status, next payment date, payment frequency, remaining payments, case, and plan status.
- **Every live plan is included, regardless of any date range on the dashboard.** A date range narrows the projected-revenue figures shown on the page; it does not narrow the export.
- Canceled plans are left out. Active, completed, and failed plans are all included.
- Remaining payments is the number of installments still needed to clear the outstanding balance at the plan's current installment size.

## Edge Cases & Limitations

- Downloading the export requires finance access. A team member without it is refused.
