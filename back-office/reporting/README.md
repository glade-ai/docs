# Reporting

## Overview

Reporting provides your firm with operational and financial visibility into your practice. The system includes workflow-level reports (intake status, paralegal workload, documents team activity, court calendar), financial reports (sales overview, conversion metrics), and task efficiency analytics. Reports support date-range filtering, team member filtering, and CSV export.

## Topics

- [Intake Status Report](./intake-status-report.md) — primary cases in a date range with consultation date, intake lead, paralegal, and recommended case type.
- [Paralegal Report](./paralegal-report.md) — per-paralegal workload, filing rate, and custom-status breakdown.
- [Documents Report](./documents-report.md) — documents team and paralegal checklist reviews and clients served.
- [Court Calendar](./court-calendar.md) — timed court hearings from PACER docket data.
- [Court Notices Report](./court-notices-report.md) — one row per PACER notice, including notices never linked to a case.
- [Bookings Report](./bookings-report.md) — one row per booking, with case assignees, lead source, labels, and client address/county.
- [Financial Reports](./financial-reports.md) — Sales Overview, Current Sales Overview, and Conversion Metrics (Firm Overview).
- [Task Efficiency and Leaderboard](./performance-analytics.md) — task time-to-completion analytics and PACER attorney filing data.
- [Dashboards](./dashboards.md) — firm-shared named dashboards and default homepage widgets.
- [Custom Reports](./custom-reports/README.md) — firm-built reports over case and payment data: filters, columns, exports, saved-report management, and time from retainer to filing.

## Configuration

- **Date range**: All workflow reports require or accept a start date and end date.
